# Fibonacci Number

**Status:** Accepted
**Language:** cpp
**Date:** 2025-12-05

## Learning Reflection
Implemented dynamic programming solution to efficiently calculate the nth Fibonacci number, leveraging memoization to store previously computed results and avoid redundant calculations.

This approach showcases the trade-off between space complexity and time complexity, highlighting the benefits of caching intermediate results in recursive algorithms.

The dynamic programming strategy employed here can be applied to other combinatorial problems where overlapping subproblems arise, demonstrating its versatility in solving optimization problems.
