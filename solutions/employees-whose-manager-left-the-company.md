# Employees Whose Manager Left the Company

**Status:** Accepted
**Language:** mysql
**Date:** 2025-12-05

## Learning Reflection
Improved my understanding of joins and subqueries by leveraging a LEFT JOIN to combine employee and manager tables, followed by a subquery to filter out employees whose manager still exists in the company. This solution showcases the importance of carefully evaluating conditions for the LEFT JOIN to achieve the desired results. The use of a subquery also exemplifies a common technique for filtering data based on complex conditions in SQL.
