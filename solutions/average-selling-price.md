# Average Selling Price

**Status:** Accepted
**Language:** mysql
**Date:** 2025-12-05

## Learning Reflection
Implemented a SQL query to calculate the average selling price of items, utilizing the concept of aggregation in SQL to group items by category and calculate the total revenue, then dividing by the total number of items to obtain the average selling price.

This solution demonstrates a basic understanding of SQL's aggregation functions, specifically SUM and COUNT, and how they can be combined to solve a practical problem.

Through this problem, I gained experience in writing efficient SQL queries that can handle large datasets and perform complex calculations, further solidifying my understanding of database querying.
